About Us

Location-aware technologies and big data are transforming the ways we study and understand human behavior. At MSL@PolyU, our mission is to pioneer geospatial solutions to propel human mobility science, a burgeoning field that seeks to model and predict patterns of movement, as well as influence the ways people navigate through urban environments and the broader earth system.

Mobility Sensing & Analytics

To improve the understanding of human and vehicle mobility using location-aware technologies and big data analytics.

Mobility Prediction & Demand Forecast

To make timely and reliable forecast of individual and collective mobility using geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI).

Behavior Change & Modelling

To understand short- and long-term changes of mobility behavior in the context of global change and new urban crises (e.g., pandemic, natural disasters).

Shared & Autonomous Mobility

To study the impact of shared mobility and autonomous vehicles on future urban systems.

Cities, Society & Connectivity

To understand the broad implications of mobility for urban development and human interactions.

Tourist Mobility & Smart Tourism

To understand tourist spatial behavior and destination choices & Develop solutions for smart and sustainable tourism development.
