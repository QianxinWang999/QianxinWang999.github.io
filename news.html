Our RSE paper on subsidence spatial pattern detection has been selected as an ESI Highly Cited Paper!

In this study, we propose a bidirectional gated recurrent unit (BiGRU) model to correct random and seasonal ADs adaptively. The BiGRU model decomposes InSAR time-series measurements into non-seasonal and seasonal components by adopting a branched network structure and extracting component-wise features separately. To remove seasonal ADs and meanwhile preserve true seasonal deformation, a dense and fully connected layer with weighted feature learning was designed.
